# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release planning, deployment readiness, and communication so changes can move from development to production with clear ownership and reduced operational risk.

### Responsibilities
- Own release calendars and deployment windows
- Coordinate pre-release validation and launch readiness checks
- Prepare rollback or contingency plans for production changes
- Align release notes, stakeholder communication, and launch timing
- Facilitate go/no-go decisions with project and engineering leads

### Goals
- Reduce deployment risk and production surprises
- Keep release activity predictable and well-communicated
- Ensure operational readiness before go-live

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and go/no-go checkpoints
- Deployment status updates and stakeholder notifications
- Post-release check-ins and incident follow-up when needed

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to align launch timing and stakeholder updates
- Collaborates with Product Managers on feature readiness and release sequencing
- Coordinates with Developers and Technical Leads to confirm implementation quality and rollback readiness
- Partners with QA/Test Leads on sign-off criteria and defect severity before production deployment
- Engages Security/Compliance Leads for release-signoff requirements and risk review

---

## Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide engineering direction, maintain technical quality, and help the team make sound trade-offs as work moves from concept to delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Define or guide technical architecture and implementation approach
- Review design decisions, dependencies, and engineering trade-offs
- Support developers with technical problem-solving and escalation paths
- Help ensure code quality, maintainability, and platform alignment
- Track technical risks and coordinate remediation planning

### Goals
- Deliver robust, maintainable solutions that fit the roadmap
- Reduce rework and avoid avoidable architecture drift
- Align engineering execution with product and delivery goals

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and technical decision meetings
- Dependency planning and architecture alignment discussions
- Technical risk and escalation updates with project leadership

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to balance customer value, technical feasibility, and roadmap priorities
- Supports Project Managers by clarifying delivery dependencies and sequencing risks
- Guides Developers on standards, architecture direction, and quality expectations
- Collaborates with QA/Test Leads on testability and defect triage
- Coordinates with Security/Compliance Leads on secure design requirements and compliance implications

---

## QA / Test Leads

### Role Summary
QA/Test Leads define quality standards, validate release readiness, and help the team reduce defects by ensuring acceptance criteria and customer needs are tested before production release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, coverage plans, and quality gates
- Validate acceptance criteria against user and business requirements
- Prioritize defect triage and regression testing efforts
- Coordinate UAT, smoke tests, and release validation activities
- Provide quality recommendations for go/no-go decisions

### Goals
- Catch issues early and reduce production risk
- Improve confidence in release quality and feature readiness
- Maintain clear, measurable quality standards across the lifecycle

### Typical Communication
- Test planning and readiness reviews
- Defect triage and regression-update meetings
- Release quality signoff and launch dependency discussions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Developers to define testable acceptance criteria and validate fixes
- Works with Product Managers to confirm that outcomes and user needs are being validated
- Supports Project Managers and Release Managers on milestone readiness and release signoff
- Coordinates with Technical Leads on testability, automation, and high-risk areas
- Escalates unresolved quality or compliance concerns to Security/Compliance Leads when relevant

---

## Security / Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads protect the organization by ensuring projects meet required controls, risk standards, and privacy expectations before release.

### Responsibilities
- Review project requirements for security, privacy, and compliance impacts
- Identify and mitigate security risks in design, data handling, and deployment
- Support secure implementation standards and governance reviews
- Ensure required controls are documented and validated before launch
- Coordinate remediation plans for escalated issues or incidents

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk across projects
- Maintain trust, governance, and operational resilience
- Prevent avoidable customer or regulatory issues

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews, access and control assessments, and compliance checkpoints
- Risk escalation meetings and remediation updates
- Release gating and incident-related follow-up

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Advises Product Managers and Project Managers on risk trade-offs and delivery constraints
- Collaborates with Technical Leads and Developers on secure design and implementation practices
- Works with QA/Test Leads to confirm security checks and validation coverage
- Supports Release Managers in launch readiness and risk-based go/no-go decisions

---

## Customer Success / Stakeholder Liaison

### Role Summary
Customer Success or Stakeholder Liaisons connect delivery teams with customer and stakeholder needs so decisions reflect real-world usage, expectations, and adoption goals.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and summarize feedback from customers, stakeholders, and support channels
- Communicate status updates and major milestones in plain language
- Help translate stakeholder priorities into product and delivery decisions
- Support adoption planning and change communication where needed
- Identify risk areas related to user impact, trust, or satisfaction

### Goals
- Improve customer experience and stakeholder confidence
- Ensure delivery decisions reflect user needs and business context
- Support smoother transitions from planning to adoption

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder briefings and customer feedback reviews
- Milestone updates and launch communication
- Adoption, readiness, and post-release feedback loops

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provides Product Managers with customer insight and feedback signals
- Helps Project Managers communicate scope, risks, and milestone expectations
- Coordinates with Release Managers on launch messaging and stakeholder readiness
- Works with Developers and Technical Leads to clarify user pain points and feature impact
- Supports QA/Test Leads by surfacing user-impact scenarios and acceptance concerns

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these roles create a clearer accountability model for planning, execution, release, and stakeholder communication.
